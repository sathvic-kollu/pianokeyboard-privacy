Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zoopet, the developer of Piano Keyboard: Learn Songs ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the mobile application Piano Keyboard: Learn Songs (package app.pianokeyboard.learnsongs, the "App"), available on Google Play and the Samsung Galaxy Store.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Your Playing Stays on Your Device

The App does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

  • The piano sounds are audio samples built into the App and played on your device. The song lessons use melodies built into the App.
  • The App does not use the microphone and never records sound.
  • Your lesson progress (stars), favourite songs and settings (keyboard size, tune volume and speed, note names, vibration, highlighting, autoscroll, note counter, the song list and group you last chose, learn mode on/off, and MIDI on/off), your practice streak (a day count and the day you last played a song correctly), and whether you have finished the welcome screens are stored only in the App's private storage.
  • If you turn on MIDI input in Options and connect a MIDI keyboard, the App reads the notes you play on it to press the on-screen keys, on your device only. MIDI data is not stored or sent anywhere. MIDI input is off by default.
  • If you use Record, the App saves the keys you play (note names and timing), not sound, under the name you give the recording, in the App's private storage. You can open and play your recordings in the App, and delete one by long-pressing it in the list.
  • None of this is uploaded. We do not receive it, we do not host it, and we cannot see it.
  • The App also stores, only on your device, whether Remove Ads is active and ad-frequency and unlock counters. The consent form stores your consent choice on your device too; the advertising SDKs read it to decide which advertising may be requested (sections 3 and 4).
  • After a correct song, the result screen can show a Share button. If you tap it, the App opens the Android share sheet with a short text: the App's name, a link to its store page, the song title (or just "a song" for one of your own recordings) and, after a Check, how many notes in a row you got right. You choose which app, if any, receives it; the App does not send it itself.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

The App itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and runs no server. The advertising SDKs listed in section 3 may collect the following, for advertising, ad measurement (analytics), and fraud prevention, and only as far as the consent you gave in the consent form (section 4) allows:

  • Advertising identifier: your device's resettable Advertising ID.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical details.
  • IP address and approximate location: the advertising SDKs may derive a city- or region-level location from your IP address. The App has no location permission and never uses precise location.
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics: ad impressions, clicks, and the advertising SDKs' own performance data.
  • Identifiers and purchase activity: a user or installation ID, in-app purchase activity, and (for Unity Ads) app usage times.

2. Purchases

The App offers a single one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". It is processed by Google Play (Google Play version) or Samsung In-App Purchase (Galaxy Store version). We never see or store your payment details. The App stores only a local flag on your device saying that the purchase is active, and it asks the store to restore it.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Services

  • Google AdMob, the advertising network with mediation. It serves the banner, interstitial, native, and app-open advertisements in the App. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents the consent form that determines whether, and which, advertising may be shown to you.
  • Google Play Billing (Google Play version only) processes the Remove Ads purchase.

3.2 Advertising Mediation Partner

AdMob mediation may route an advertising request to the following network, which has its own privacy policy for the data it processes. For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Unity Ads receives your consent choice through the consent signals that the consent form stores, passed on by Google's mediation adapter.

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Privacy Policy

3.3 Samsung Galaxy Store Version

The version of the App distributed through the Samsung Galaxy Store uses Samsung In-App Purchase instead of Google Play Billing to process the Remove Ads purchase. Google Play Billing is not present in that version. Samsung Privacy Policy

4. Advertising and Your Consent

The App is supported by advertising. Before any advertisement is requested, the App asks the Google User Messaging Platform whether consent is required in your region. Where it is, including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are shown a consent form, and no advertising request is made until you have answered it. If you refuse consent to personalised advertising, advertising is either non-personalised or not shown at all, depending on your answer.

Where your region requires it, you can change your choices at any time in the App's settings under Options > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

After you buy Remove Ads, the App makes no further advertising requests.

5. Permissions the App Requests

  • Internet and network state: used only to load advertising and for the store purchase. Playing and lessons work offline.
  • Advertising ID: as described in section 4.
  • Vibration: used only for the optional key-press vibration, which you can turn off in Options.
  • Wake lock: used by the advertising SDK's background scheduler.
  • Ad services (Android Privacy Sandbox): lets the advertising SDKs use the device's on-device advertising APIs (Topics, attribution); you can control these in your device's privacy settings.
  • Store billing (Google Play or Samsung, depending on the version): used for the Remove Ads purchase.

The App does not request microphone, storage, media, camera, location, contacts, or account permissions.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in your device's application settings, removes all lesson progress, recordings, practice streak, favourites and settings stored by the App. The App's data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

7.2 Withdrawing Advertising Consent

Use Options > Privacy options in the App's settings (where offered), reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings, or buy Remove Ads, which stops all advertising requests.

7.3 Regional Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a jurisdiction with comparable law, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection for personal data we hold. Because we hold none of your progress, recordings, favourites or settings, most such requests concern only the advertising data processed by the third parties in section 3. Write to us and we will direct you to the correct controller.

8. Data Retention

We keep no copy of your progress, recordings, favourites or settings, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Correspondence you send to our support address is kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies, linked in section 3.

9. Security

Your progress, recordings, favourites and settings are stored in the App's private storage, which other applications on your device cannot read. Network traffic from the advertising and billing services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
